BEFORE YOU GO

A few useful checks.

  • Pack waterproofs, warm layers and grippy footwear. Wet leaves and waterfall paths can be slippery.
  • Download maps before leaving: signal can be patchy. This app is not offline navigation.
  • Finish woodland trails by 15:30 and aim to leave by 16:30. A conservative daylight plan, not a verified sunset time.
  • Use signed parking, check charges and never block entrances or passing places. The south Loch Tay road is narrow in places.
  • Waterfall routes are steep and uneven, not step-free. The gentler option is not an audited accessible route.
